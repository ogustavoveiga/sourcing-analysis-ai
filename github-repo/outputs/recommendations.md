# Recruiter Recommendations

*Based on analysis of 600 candidates · Generated via Claude Sonnet*

---

## Priority 1 — Fix Outreach Before Adding Volume
**Owner:** Recruiter lead + all recruiters
**Timeline:** Immediate

Over 60% of all rejections are engagement-driven — candidates who never properly entered the process. Improving outreach quality will have more impact than sourcing more candidates.

**Actions:**
- Audit outreach messages for LinkedIn (37% no-response), Hunting (36%), and Indicação (40%)
- A/B test subject lines and message length on LinkedIn campaigns
- Set a rule: if no response after 2 follow-ups over 10 days, archive the candidate
- Track response rate as a KPI alongside hire rate

**Expected impact:** 15% improvement in response rate → ~30 additional active candidates per cycle

---

## Priority 2 — Realign Recruiters to Their Best Channels
**Owner:** Talent Acquisition Manager
**Timeline:** Next cycle planning

Every recruiter in the team converts at least 2× better in their best channel than their overall rate. The current channel assignment appears random rather than strength-based.

**Actions:**
- Assign Fernanda primarily to Hunting (her best channel: 8.3% vs 3.9% overall)
- Assign Diego primarily to Hunting (best channel: 11.8% vs 4.9% overall)
- Reduce Gustavo's total volume and focus him on Github (14.3% vs 5.0% overall)
- Keep Bruno on Banco de Talentos (28.6% — already his primary channel)

**Expected impact:** Channel realignment could lift team hire rate from 8% to ~11% without new headcount

---

## Priority 3 — Create a Response-Time SLA for Candidates
**Owner:** All recruiters (process change)
**Timeline:** Immediate

Candidates who respond in 3–6 days convert at 15.3% — 53% higher than fast responders (10.1%) and 54% higher than slow ones (9.9%). These are the highest-intent candidates and they deserve the fastest internal process.

**Actions:**
- Flag candidates who respond in 3–6 days for same-day internal review
- Deprioritize (but do not discard) candidates who respond after 7+ days
- Measure and report "time from candidate response to recruiter next step" weekly

**Expected impact:** Shifting recruiter effort toward medium-response candidates improves efficiency per hour without changing headcount

---

## Priority 4 — Set Score Thresholds Before Generating Offers
**Owner:** Hiring managers + recruiters
**Timeline:** Before next offer cycle

The Test → Offer stage has the steepest drop in the funnel (30.5%). This suggests offers may be generated without consistent score benchmarks, leading to wasted time on low-probability candidates.

**Actions:**
- Use technical_test_score ≥ 78 and behavior_score ≥ 78 as soft thresholds before advancing to offer
- Document exceptions when scores are below threshold (special circumstances)
- Share score averages for hired candidates with all hiring managers as calibration reference

**Expected impact:** Reduces wasted offer generation and focuses closing effort on highest-fit candidates

---

## Priority 5 — Reduce Investment in Low-ROI Channels
**Owner:** Talent Acquisition Manager
**Timeline:** Next quarter budget review

Evento (4.9% hire rate) and Indicação (3.2% hire rate) together account for 145 candidates but only 6 hires. The same sourcing effort on Github or Inbound would have yielded significantly more.

**Actions:**
- Reduce Evento participation unless roles are specifically suited to event sourcing
- Rethink Indicação process — consider adding a structured pre-screening before introducing to the pipeline
- Reallocate saved time to Inbound optimization (highest ROI channel: 10.1% hire rate + lowest ghost rate)

**Expected impact:** Redirecting 145 sourcing slots to higher-yield channels could double the hires from those slots

---

## Composite High-Probability Profile

A candidate with all the following attributes converts at dramatically higher than the 8% base rate:

- Sourced via Github or Inbound
- Seniority: Senior or Staff
- Department: Tech or Data
- Work mode: Híbrido
- Response time: 3–6 days
- Technical score ≥ 78
- Behavior score ≥ 78
- Manager score ≥ 78
- Years experience ≥ 6

**Recruiter action:** When a candidate matches 7+ of these criteria, prioritize immediately and reduce time-to-offer.

---

## Composite Low-Probability Profile

A candidate with the following attributes is unlikely to convert based on dataset patterns:

- Sourced via Indicação or Evento
- Seniority: Junior
- Work mode: Remoto
- Response time: 7+ days (or no response)
- Technical score < 73
- Department: Operations

**Recruiter action:** Do not invest more than one follow-up attempt. Archive after 10 days of no engagement.
